How it works, and how it was checked

Every change to the model was tested on seasons it had not seen (trained on 2022 to 2024, tested on 2025 and 2026) before it was adopted.

Win-probability error (lower is better)

A ball-by-ball simulation engine replaced the original model because it predicts later seasons better.

Do the ratings repeat? (higher is better)

Agreement between ratings built from two random halves of the matches.

What helps predict the next ball

Delivery type and match context clearly help predict a ball. They did not make the player rankings more reliable, so the final ranking keeps the simpler opposition-quality adjustment. Grey = no measurable gain.

Who is rated

At least 150 pressure balls (100 in IPL or top-ten T20I matches), 15 matches, and active in 2025 or 2026. Stronger competitions and recent seasons count more.

Limits

Ratings from a few hundred balls are noisy, so ranks below about sixth are close. Most pressure falls in the chase. Line and length, dew, weather and fielding are not in the data and were not assumed.
